## Research Analysis & Next Steps

### 1. Analysis of Current State
In Phase 12, we achieved exceptional predictive performance and accelerated adaptation by successfully mounting Closed-Loop Thalamic Subsumption Motorics (CLTS) on top of our non-parametric representation base (Arm F). However, we identified a **marginal representation drift** under active CLTS control: the centroid decoding MSE of the novel object reached **85.85**, slightly exceeding our falsification threshold of **85.0**. This indicates that intense physical contact and active collisions introduce slight representational degradation when the model is forced to coordinate its behavior without explicit spatial grounding.

### 2. Architectural Pivot: Pixel-Position Encodings
To address this "Spatial Specialization Gap," Phase 13 will implement the **Extended Input Model (RGB + Pixel-Position)**. By providing explicit spatial coordinate keys alongside RGB values, we expect the convolutional backbone to construct structurally grounded representations that are immune to representation drift under active control.

We will compare two distinct positional encoding strategies:
- **Linear Normalized Encoding (Arm H):** A simple coordinate channel mapping position lineally.
- **Sinusoidal Encoding (Arm I):** Multi-frequency sinusoidal embeddings mapping position non-linearly to provide multi-scale coordinate resolution.

### 3. Verification Protocol
Both architectures will be evaluated under the complete CLTS active probing paradigm over a 5-seed comparative sweep to control for run-to-run variance, ensuring rigorous and scientific validation.
