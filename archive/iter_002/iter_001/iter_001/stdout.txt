### 2. JEPA Architecture with GDASR and VICReg (`src/models.py`)
- **Encoder:** A 1D Convolutional Neural Network mapping inputs of shape `(B, 3, 128)` to a latent space of shape `(B, D_max)` where $D_{\max} = 8$.
- **Predictor:** An MLP that maps a history of active latent states `(B, H * D_max)` where $H = 3$ to a prediction of the next latent state $z_{t+1}$ of shape `(B, D_max)`. Inactive dimensions are zeroed/padded dynamically.
- **FixedJEPA:** Baseline model utilizing a fixed representational subspace dimension ($d_t = 2$ or $3$).
- **DynamicJEPA (GDASR):** Dynamically recruits new dimensions based on the Gradient-Driven Active Subspace Recruitment algorithm.
  - Starts with $d_t = 2$.
  - Tracks the EMA of prediction error.
  - Triggers recruitment if the error exceeds `mean + 4 * std` calculated over a stable 2-object baseline, subject to a cooldown period ($N_{\text{cooldown}} = 500$).
- **VICReg Loss Implementation:**
  - **Invariance loss:** MSE of prediction in active latent dimensions.
  - **Variance loss:** Forces the standard deviation of each active dimension across the batch to be $\ge 1$ (using biased standard deviation to safely prevent NaNs with small/single-batch updates).
  - **Covariance loss:** Minimizes off-diagonal cross-correlations between active dimensions.
- **Stabilization & Stop-Gradients:** When a new dimension is recruited, existing dimensions ($0$ to $d_t - 2$) are frozen with respect to current loss optimization for a stabilization period ($200$ steps) by detaching the outputs of the encoder and predictor along those dimensions.
