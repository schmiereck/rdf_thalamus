### 1. Implemented Components
I wrote high-quality, robust, and well-commented PyTorch classes in `src/thalamus.py`:
- **`SegmentEncoder(d_max=8)`**: A 1D CNN that maps segment shapes of `(B, 3, 32)` to latent representation vectors of size `(B, d_max)`.
- **`SegmentPredictor(d_max=8, h=3)`**: An MLP that predicts the next segment latent state from the history of `h` previous states.
- **`L2Encoder(d_max=8)`**: An MLP mapping the concatenated/aggregated active L1 latents of all 4 segments (total size `4 * d_max`) to L2 latent space of size `d_max`.
- **`L2Predictor(d_max=8, h=3)`**: Predicts the next L2 latent state from the history of `h` previous L2 states.
- **`ThalamusNet(d_max=8, h=3, cooldown=200)`**:
  - Implements the 4 local L1 segments and 1 global L2 layer.
  - Implements local temporal surprise calculation ($||z - \hat{z}||^2$) for each of the 5 loci.
  - **Surprise Watchdog**: Tracks the running EMA of the mean and variance of raw surprise for all 5 loci, and applies Soft-Z-Score Normalization using `var_floor=1e-4` and `epsilon=1e-4`.
  - **Robust Relative Stability Lock**: Tracks overall L1 surprise EMA and L1 minimum surprise. If the overall L1 surprise is greater than $\theta_{conv} = \max(0.25, 1.5 \times \text{L1\_surprise\_min})$, L2 is locked.
  - **Token Routing & Cooldown**: Handles active token locus in $\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$ with a 200-step cooldown.
  - **Plasticity Gating**: Dynamically sets `requires_grad = True/False` on L1/L2 and zeroes out inactive gradients to ensure that only the active layer holding the token undergoes gradient updating.
  - **Priming & Self-Generation**: Supports both external RGB query vectors and self-generated queries from a linear `color_readout` layer mapping L2 prediction with a stop-gradient. Segment color similarities to the query are computed and added to bias raw L1 surprises.
  - **Physical Tracking Overlap**: Translates the active attention locus into the 1D physical segment space and returns `1.0` if it aligns with the target object's 32-pixel segment, else `0.0`.
- **`NonGatedControlNet(d_max=8, h=3)`**:
