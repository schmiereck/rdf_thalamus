However, this positional-shortcut hypothesis was resoundingly falsified; the coordinate channels degraded the optimization landscape, increased prediction error, and failed to prevent coordinate drift because the network exploited the raw coordinate input as a shortcut rather than learning the underlying physics.

Instead of modifying the input (which creates shortcuts), CCR applies self-supervised contrastive constraints directly onto the latent coordinates generated by the soft-argmax bottleneck.
