   - **Stage 1**:
     - Automatically sets `requires_grad = True` on all model parameters.
     - Does **not** detach the input `x` in `forward_spatial` before `conv_spatial_coord`.
     - Does **not** detach `z_hist_coord` or `z_target_coord` before passing to the predictor or in the similarity loss, allowing prediction gradients to flow all the way back to the coordinate head and backbone `conv1-4`.
   - **Stage 2**:
     - Automatically sets `requires_grad = False` on the parameters of `encoder.conv_spatial_coord`.
     - Detaches the backbone feature map `x` before passing it to `conv_spatial_coord` inside `forward_spatial`.
     - Inject stop-gradients via `.detach()` at the output of the coordinate stream before feeding into the predictor (making `z_hist_coord` and `z_target_coord` detached inputs/targets).
     - Keeps the dynamics stream and predictor fully trainable.
