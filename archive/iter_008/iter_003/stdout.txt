### 3. Key Observations & Analysis

1. **Unsupervised Spatial Localization ($\lambda > 0$):**
   Adding the soft spatial variance penalty ($\lambda$) dramatically narrows the activation profile. In the unconstrained **Control**, the spatial variance of the recruited channel is extremely wide (**1209.33**). Introducing the standard bottleneck penalty ($\lambda = 0.1$) reduces spatial variance by **95.0%** down to **60.29**. Under a strong bottleneck ($\lambda = 1.0$), it falls to **31.08**.

2. **Representation Collapse Prevention:**
   Without the bottleneck constraint, the model frequently suffers from representation collapse (Criterion 5), where the recruited channel becomes inactive or loses temporal variability. This occurs in **40% of Control seeds** and **20% of Exp $\lambda = 0$ seeds**. Under any non-zero spatial bottleneck ($\lambda \in \{0.01, 0.1, 1.0\}$), **collapse rates fall to 0% across all seeds**, showing that spatial regularizers actively promote representation stability.

3. **Trade-offs with Coordinate Alignment:**
   A mild bottleneck ($\lambda = 0.01$) produces the most accurate post-hoc centroid-to-physical coordinate linear decoding (**MSE = 69.11**), whereas increasing regularizations ($\lambda = 0.1 \to 1.0$) slightly increases the decoding MSE. This indicates a trade-off where excessively constraining the spatial footprint can restrict the spatial representation's flexibility for path integration.
