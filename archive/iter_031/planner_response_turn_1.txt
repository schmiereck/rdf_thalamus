def calculate_centroid_and_variance(a_spatial):
    """
    Computes spatial centroid and soft spatial variance for each channel.
    Args:

            def calc_var_loss(x, gamma=1.0, eps=1e-4):
                mean = x.mean(dim=0)
                var = torch.mean((x - mean) ** 2, dim=0)

analysis: |
  This phase tested the ONE remaining hypothesis that could have rescued M2: that
  Reconstruction+VICReg would achieve ΔR²_color ≥ 0.30, establishing a ceiling for
  identity encoding and showing the decoder-free constraint was the bottleneck.
  
  The result is a comprehensive architectural null:
  
  1. Reconstruction DOES reconstruct pixels well (MSE=0.018), confirming the spatial
     features a_dyn contain the information. But mean-pooling z_dyn = a_dyn.mean(dim=-1)
     is a spatial low-pass filter that destroys per-object color identity. Color varies
     across SPATIAL positions (object 0 is at position 30, object 1 at 70, etc.), and
     averaging across space mixes all object colors into a single scalar per channel.
  
  2. The d_max=2 control (F3) was within 0.036 of d_max=8 — channel count is irrelevant
     because the bottleneck is spatial, not channel-level. Even 2 channels with 8 spatial
     positions each (16 scalars) can reconstruct 384 pixel values; the 64 scalars at
     d_max=8 add nothing to identity because identity is lost in the spatial average.
  
  3. The random-encoder control (Arm C) collapsed 100% — VICReg needs gradient flow
     through the encoder to maintain per-dimension variance. Without training, the
     encoder produces near-zero outputs. This means F4 is uninterpretable (we cannot
     distinguish "training matters for identity" from "training matters for viability"),
     but it confirms that VICReg's variance term is load-bearing for representation
     existence, not just quality.
  
  4. The CLTS calibration (Part B) shows that the representation quality constrains
     downstream motor behavior: even with collision-sparse environments, channels that
     don't track individual objects can't produce selective attention. The directional
     signal in collision selectivity (0.59 vs 0.44) suggests the surprise mechanism
     works when the representation cooperates, but the VICReg-only representation
     doesn't reliably cooperate.
  
  Pre-committed mandate revision (from pre-registration):
  "Reconstruction+VICReg fails to achieve ΔR²_color ≥ 0.30 with variance-stability.
  Even a supervised pixel-reconstruction target cannot make the mean-readout z_dyn
  stream encode identity above the 0.30 threshold. The z_dyn readout architecture
  itself constrains identity encoding regardless of objective class. M2 revision
  pending architectural redesign: priority is centroid-gated z_dyn readout or
  increased d_max."
  
  The centroid-gated readout from iter_027 Arm A' is the most promising architectural
  redesign because it reads z_dyn at the POSITION of each centroid (z_coord), rather
  than averaging across all positions. This should preserve per-object color
  information because it samples the spatial features at object locations.
