def calculate_centroid_and_variance(a_spatial):
    """
    Computes spatial centroid and soft spatial variance for each channel.
    Args:
        a_spatial (Tensor): shape (B, d_max, 128)
    Returns:
        x_mean (Tensor): shape (B, d_max)
        var (Tensor): shape (B, d_max)

1. **Root cause hypothesis:** In the current `NonParametricEncoder`, both `z_coord` (soft-argmax centroid) and `z_dyn` (mean/centroid-gated readout) are derived from the SAME shared CNN backbone (conv1→conv2→conv3→conv4). This explains both the collapse (VICReg can't maintain variance when competing with the much larger sim_loss gradient) and the train-vs-eval discrepancy (training maintains variance on the training distribution through VICReg, but the narrow representation doesn't generalize to eval data).
