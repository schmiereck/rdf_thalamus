The iter_021 interpretation "z_dyn changes MORE than z_coord" is technically correct but misleading — z_coord's near-zero delta is a tracking failure, not evidence that z_coord is properly slow.

2. **CGIR Threshold (Issue 2):** Pre-registered C3 was `delta_R2_color(Arm A) ≥ 0.10` (absolute). Arm A=+0.050 → FAIL. But improvement(A-B)=+0.124 → would PASS an improvement criterion. The criterion was poorly designed. Use improvement-based thresholds going forward.

3. **Architectural Ceiling (Issue 3):** z_dyn[c] = 1 scalar × d_t=3 channels = 3 scalars for 3 objects × 4 identity features (R,G,B,radius) = 12 values. Information-theoretically impossible. The negative delta_R2_identity (-0.035) across ALL arms is consistent with this bottleneck. CGIR's partial improvement (+0.124 on color) confirms spatial-mean was a contributing factor, but the 1-scalar ceiling prevents full identity encoding.
