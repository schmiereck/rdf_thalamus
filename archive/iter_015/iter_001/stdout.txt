## Phase 15 — Findings Report

### Aggregate means (5 seeds, final step 3000 evaluation, N=4 environment with 2× mass perturbation)

| Arm | mse_cent | test_sim_loss | mean_var_3 | final d_t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K (Baseline, fixed CCR-Cov) | 62.64 | 0.090 | 8.28 | 4.0 |
| L (Proportional SA-CCR) | 65.53 | 14.88 | 8.13 | 4.2 |
| M (Inverse SA-CCR) | 64.08 | 0.148 | 8.22 | 4.0 |
| N (Dual Control) | 130.39 | 0.167 | 33.78 | 3.0 |

### Pre-registered falsification audit

- **C1 (L > K on MSE, p < 0.05): FALSIFIED.** Arm L mse_cent = 65.53 vs Arm K = 62.64; Welch p = 0.895. The proportional surprise schedule does not deliver a statistically significant improvement in post-collision centroid decoding.
- **C2 (L sim_loss statistically inferior to K): NOT falsified.** Welch p = 0.373; the elevated mean (14.88) is driven by a single seed-456 outlier (sim_loss = 73.97), so the difference does not survive Welch's test.
- **C3 (Arm L var > 8.5): NOT falsified.** mean_var_3 = 8.13.
- **C4 (M strictly better than L on both): FALSIFIED.** Arm M has lower MSE (64.08 < 65.53) *and* lower sim_loss (0.148 < 14.88). Inverse scaling is at least as good as proportional scaling.

**Overall SA-CCR hypothesis: FALSIFIED.**

### Strategic Manager's empirical test (Arm N)

The slow Categorizer with the consistency-ratio criterion `L_consistency = sim_loss_new / sim_loss_old` **mathematically cannot accept any dimension recruitment** in this architecture: the predictor mask zeros out outputs beyond `d_t`, so the cold dimension yields `sim_loss_new ≈ mse(0, target)` in the thousands while `sim_loss_old ≈ 0.2`. Across all 5 seeds the passive 2→3 transition was suppressed (ratios 13,705–37,078) and the 31 active-phase retries (1800, 1850, …, 3000) all rejected (ratios growing from ~3,600 to ~33,000). The embedded GDASR fast detector still recruited 2→3 in active training, so the model reached d_t = 3 but never d_t = 4, leaving the 4th object untracked (`mean_var_3` = 33.78, three to four times the CCR arms).

This is a *structural* empirical finding, not an artifact of tuning: a vanilla MDL-style consistency gate cannot promote a dimension whose predictor head has never seen non-zero gradient. Future Dual-Control implementations need a warm-up: train the new dim's predictor head for a short window before evaluating consistency.

### Interpretation
1. **SA-CCR is a null result.** Dynamic scaling of `λ_cov(t)` by smoothed surprise — in either direction — adds no measurable benefit over the fixed Phase-14 baseline. Inverse scaling is even slightly preferred, which is consistent with the Manager's warning that surprise-proportional regularization is a "soft patch."
2. **Proportional SA-CCR exhibits a destabilization mode.** On seed 456 the amplified covariance penalty drove the loss to 73.97 and triggered runaway recruitment to d_t = 5, while neighbouring arms stayed at d_t = 4. The proportional schedule is therefore not just neutral but carries tail risk.
3. **Strict consistency gating is too strict** without a warm-up — the gate can never fire. This is exactly the empirical regularity the Manager solicited.
