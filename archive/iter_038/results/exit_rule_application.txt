2. THE EXIT RULE BEING APPLIED
================================================================================

From pre-registration Section 3, Step 5:

"The hypothesis is falsified if ANY gate fails under the per-seed decision rule.
If any gate fails, the behavioral-validation strategy is declared not tractable
within project scope, and the project pivots to path (ii) re-frame."

Gate-1b FAILs because std(CV) = 0.320 exceeds the threshold of 0.25. This means
the heterogeneity, while present (mean CV = 1.025 >> 0.30), is not reproducible
across seeds — it varies wildly from seed to seed, dominated by trajectory luck
rather than structural spatial effects. The per-seed CVs [0.817, 1.414, 0.771,
0.707, 1.414] show a bimodal pattern (two seeds at ~0.75, two seeds at ~1.41,
one at ~0.82), indicating that the random walk's spatial clustering is highly
sensitive to initial object placement and trajectory history, not a stable
structural property of the dynamics.

This is the same failure mode identified in iter_037 (per-seed CVs were
[0.00, 1.41, 0.82, 0.00, 1.41] with std=0.62), though attenuated. The navigation
design did not sufficiently decouple event generation from trajectory luck to
produce reproducible heterogeneity.
